SnapEDesign (Snap-e-Design)

Next-Gen Web-Based Interior Floor Planner & Augmented Reality (AR) Studio

1. Project Overview

SnapEDesign is an interactive web application that bridges 2D interior space planning with real-time 3D Augmented Reality (AR). It allows homeowners, designers, and clients to design room layouts and project photorealistic 3D furniture models directly into their physical room using mobile phone cameras.

📐 2D Floor Planner

Drag-and-drop 2D and 3D furniture into an interactive grid canvas. Move, rotate 45°, scale, and customize wall paint tinting in real time.

📱 Real Room AR Camera

Streams live video feed from device rear camera, overlays 3D models with 360° Pitch/Yaw/Roll rotation controls, and supports full WebXR AR.

🤝 Designer Marketplace

Connects clients with interior designers to post room requests, review project estimates, submit proposals, and save portfolio snapshots.

B. Connecting & Running on Your Mobile Phone

🔒 Why HTTPS is Required for Mobile:
Mobile browsers (Google Chrome & Safari) strictly disable camera access (navigator.mediaDevices) on insecure http:// connections. An HTTPS connection is essential for camera and AR permissions to function.
Method 1 (Recommended for Mobile AR/Camera): Public Secure Cloudflare Tunnel (HTTPS)
Open the active secure URL on your phone's browser (Chrome or Safari):
https://introducing-caps-bar-tired.trycloudflare.com
(Works anywhere over 4G/5G/Wi-Fi with zero setup; direct HTTPS with full camera & AR support).
Method 2: Local Wi-Fi Network
Connect phone to the same Wi-Fi as your PC, then navigate to: http://10.235.209.3:5173.
Note: Local HTTP may block live camera permissions on some mobile browsers unless configured in chrome://flags.

4. How to Use the App (Feature Guide)

1. Design Space (Floor Planner Canvas)

  • Drag & Drop Furniture: Drag items from the left sidebar catalog (chairs, lamps, tables, beds) onto the grid canvas.
  • Interactive Controls: Tap any furniture item on canvas to rotate it 45° or remove it.
  • Add 3D Items: Click any 3D item on the right sidebar to drop an interactive 3D model directly onto the floor plan.
  • Wall Paint Customizer: Choose wall colors from the palette to instantly change room ambience.

2. In View AR (Real Room View)

  • Activate Camera: Tap the "📷 Turn On Room Camera" button on your phone and tap "Allow" when prompted.
  • Flip Camera: Use the refresh icon in the header to switch between rear (room) and front cameras.
  • 3D Angle Drawer: Adjust Pitch (X), Yaw (Y), and Roll (Z) sliders or use presets (Front, Top 90°, Side 90°, Floor Flat).
  • In View AR in Real Room: Tap "📱 In View AR (Real Room)" to trigger WebXR or Scene Viewer for real-world plane tracking and scaling.
  • Take Snapshots: Tap the camera icon in the top right to save snapshots of your designs.

3. Designer Marketplace

Clients can post room consultation requests with photos and budgets; interior designers can accept projects and submit customized 3D design proposals.

5. Quick Troubleshooting Matrix

Problem Root Cause Solution
Camera doesn't turn on / shows simulated photo Opened site using plain HTTP (e.g. http://10.235.209.3:5173), which blocks getUserMedia. Use the secure HTTPS tunnel link (https://...trycloudflare.com) and click "Allow" on camera permissions.
3D Model appears black or takes long to load Large 3D GLB file size over slow mobile connection. Select lightweight models like Kitchen Mixer or Geo Planter, or allow a few seconds to cache.
"Vite 403 Forbidden" when opening tunnel Vite blocks unrecognized host headers by default. Resolved by setting allowedHosts: true in vite.config.js.
